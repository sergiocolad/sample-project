## Context

What is the problem? What forces (technical, product, team, timeline) are
pushing on this decision? State facts, not the conclusion.

## Decision

The single sentence that states what we're doing. If you need three
sentences, the decision probably isn't clear enough yet.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — what it is, why it lost.
- **Option B** — what it is, why it lost.

## Consequences

What gets easier. What gets harder. What we're explicitly accepting as a
tradeoff. This section is what future readers actually come here for — an
ADR that only lists upside wasn't honest about the decision.

## Revisit when

The condition under which this decision should be reopened (a scale
threshold, a new requirement, a deprecation upstream). If there is none,
say "no known trigger" rather than omitting the section.
